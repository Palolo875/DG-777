The walk from Farringdon

713 m · about 9 minutes at an easy pace · no steps recorded on the way

  1. 1Leave Farringdon by exit A onto Cowcross Street, then go north up Turnmill Street.0 m
  2. 2Turn right into Benjamin Street, which runs on as Albion Place.85 m
  3. 3Turn left up St John’s Lane.270 m
  4. 4Turn right into Passing Alley and walk through its two covered passages to St John Street.315 m
  5. 5Turn left up St John Street and cross Clerkenwell Road at the lights.425 m
  6. 6After about 180 m, turn right into Brewhouse Yard. The yard is cobbled.610 m
  7. 7Go through the passage at the far end of the yard. Unit 3 is on the ground floor: ring “Wessel Fane”.713 m

Coming to the studio

visiting arrangements and building details

Meetings
By appointment. First meetings are an hour at the models table, with drawings of comparable buildings.
Access
Step-free from the yard into a ground-floor studio, with an accessible WC. The last 100 m of the yard and Turnmill Street are paved in setts. Tell us what would make your visit easier.
By bike
Stands at the St John Street end of the yard.
